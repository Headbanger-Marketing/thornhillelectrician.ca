Before work begins

Decide which rooms stay in use before work starts.

Picture a home bought recently, where the previous owner's changes may be unknown. Its circuit labels and earlier additions then cannot be taken on trust, and the purchase inspection report may list items needing attention while the household carries on living there.

Write down which rooms, appliances and routes the household needs every day, and which can pause. Share that list with the contractor so the order of work and each switched-off circuit fit around it. Ask for the plan in writing.

How do I record what the previous owner changed?

Photograph the panel door and its labels from outside, collect the inspection report, seller disclosures and any earlier ESA paperwork, and note anything that looks altered. Do not open covers; the contractor can assess what is behind them.

Can some circuits stay on while others are being worked on?

Often the work can be sequenced so that only the affected circuits are switched off, but that depends on the existing installation. Ask the contractor to explain which areas lose supply, in what order, and how that was decided.

How should I compare quotes when the work happens in phases?

Have each contractor list the phases, the circuits switched off in each one and the finishing left between them. Quotes that describe the same phases in the same order are easier to compare fairly.

Who needs to agree before part of my house is switched off?

Anyone who shares the supply or the space, such as a co-owner, a tenant or a condominium corporation, may need notice. Name who can authorize the work and who can open the door on each day.

After I send the form, does anyone confirm my project?

Treat the form as an enquiry the site reviews, not a confirmation; the site publishes guides, arranges no visit or price and never carries out electrical work. Before agreeing, have any provider or Licensed Electrical Contractor confirm separately who they are and their licence, and check that licence through ESA. Settle scope and cost directly, and timing too.
